Step 02
Set your
operating schedule
First, tell DockSync when your dock is open. Choose working days, set open and close times, add restricted hours like breaks and shift changes, block out holidays and maintenance days, and set how far ahead suppliers get an automatic reminder. Every rule here is enforced automatically — suppliers can only ever book inside your windows.

Step 09
Driver arrives.
QR check-in.
Print your unique QR code, laminate it, and put it at the gatehouse. The driver scans it with any phone camera — no app to download. They enter their name, vehicle registration and booking token; DockSync matches it to the booking, confirms the bay, and logs the exact arrival time automatically.
Download the code from My Account → QR Code. One code covers your whole site — every scan is matched to the driver's booking token.

Step 10
Text the driver
from the dashboard
The moment a driver checks in, a text button appears next to their booking under Actions. Tap it to send an SMS straight to the mobile number they entered at the gate — tell them which bay to pull onto, ask them to wait in the marshalling area, or let them know you're running behind. No radios, no walking out to the yard.

The text action is only available once a driver has checked in — that's how DockSync has their mobile number to message.

Step 11
One tap to mark it
unloaded or collected
When the job's done, hit the green tick next to the booking in Bookings. That marks the delivery as unloaded (or the collection as collected), stamps the completion time, and records the turnaround. Your KPIs, bay availability and supplier stats all update instantly — no separate paperwork.
