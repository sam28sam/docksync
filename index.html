FAQ

Questions before
you start?

01How quickly can we get started?
Most teams can set up their first live schedule quickly: add bays, set operating hours, create bookings or share supplier booking access, then display the QR code for driver check-in.
02Can the warehouse create bookings itself?
Yes. DockSync is not limited to supplier self-booking. Your team can create bookings directly for deliveries, collections or planned dock activity.
03Is DockSync only for inbound deliveries?
No. DockSync can manage inbound deliveries, outbound collections, returns and wider dock scheduling activity.
04Do suppliers need a login?
No. Suppliers use a simple controlled booking link — no account to create and no password to manage. Your team keeps control of availability, bays and operating rules.
05Do drivers need an app?
No. Drivers scan a QR code from their smartphone.
06Can we text the driver after check-in?
Yes. Once the driver checks in and provides their mobile number, the warehouse can send short SMS instructions from the dashboard.
07Can we export our data?
Yes. DockSync supports CSV export for bookings and supplier reports, so teams can analyse or share the data outside the platform.
08What reporting does DockSync provide?
DockSync gives visibility over bookings, deliveries, collections, supplier performance, on-time rates, completion rates, missed bookings, delays, check-ins, bay activity and operational trends.
09Does DockSync replace our WMS?
No. DockSync sits before and around the WMS by controlling booking, arrival, bay scheduling and supplier visibility. It can complement existing warehouse systems.
10Is the 30-day trial really free?
Yes. No payment required, no card needed and no automatic billing at the end.
